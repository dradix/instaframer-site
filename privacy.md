# Privacy Policy

**Instant Framer** · Effective October 11, 2026 · Published by Viveros Corp

## The short version

- **Your photos never leave your phone.** Framing happens entirely on your device. We don't upload, store or see your photos.
- **You choose which photos the app can see.** Instant Framer uses Android's photo picker and does not ask for access to your whole photo library.
- **No account, no sign-in.** We don't collect your name, email or contacts (unless you email us yourself).
- **The free version shows ads from Google AdMob.** Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads, as described below. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent first.

## 1. Who we are

Instant Framer ("the app") is published by Viveros Corp ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, email [jviveroscorp@gmail.com](mailto:jviveroscorp@gmail.com).

## 2. Your photos

When you tap *Choose photos*, Android's system photo picker opens. The app can read only the photos you select there, and only to frame them. Instant Framer does not request permission to browse your photo library.

- All framing (borders, crop, colors, captions) is processed on your device.
- Framed copies are saved to your device's gallery (in an *Instant Framer* album unless you turn that off), or only to the app's private cache when you share without saving; that cache is cleared at your next export.
- Your original photos are never modified.
- By default, framed copies do **not** include your photo's metadata (EXIF data such as capture date, camera model or location). If you turn on *Keep location & camera data*, the framed copy keeps whatever metadata the original file provides. Android's photo picker may already remove location data before the app receives the photo.
- When you share to Instagram or another app, the framed images go to the app you choose and are handled under that app's privacy policy.

## 3. Data collected for advertising

The free version of Instant Framer shows ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app collects and shares the following data with Google:

| Data | Examples | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device or other IDs | Android advertising ID, app set ID | Ads, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Approximate location | Derived from your IP address | Ads, analytics, fraud prevention |
| App interactions | App launches, ad views and taps | Ads, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Diagnostics | Performance data about ad loading | Ads, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Crash logs | Crash information from the ads SDK | Ads, analytics, fraud prevention |

This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you, and we don't combine it with other data about you. Google's use of this data is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); see also [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Your choices about ads

- **Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland).** Before any ads are requested, the app shows Google's consent message, where you can consent, decline (*Do not consent*) or choose per purpose (*Manage options*). If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data for things like frequency capping and fraud prevention. You can change your choice at any time in the app under *Settings → Privacy choices for ads*.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's *Settings → Privacy → Ads* (the exact path varies by device).
- Instant Framer Pro removes all ads, so the ads SDK no longer requests ads.

## 4. What we don't collect

- Your photos or their contents
- Your name, email address, phone number or contacts (if you email us, for example with *Settings → Send feedback*, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply)
- Precise (GPS) location
- Any account information: the app has no accounts

## 5. Permissions the app uses

- **Internet and network state**: to load ads.
- **Notifications** (only asked for Instant Framer Pro background exports): to show export progress and tell you when your photos are ready. These notifications are created on your device.
- **Foreground service** (Instant Framer Pro only): to keep an export running when you leave the app.
- **Advertising ID and ad-measurement permissions** (`AD_ID`, Android ad services), added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK: to show and measure ads, as described in section 3. Instant Framer Pro stops all ad requests.
- **Google Play billing** (`BILLING`): to buy Instant Framer Pro and restore it on a new device.

The app does not request access to your photo library, camera, microphone, contacts or precise location.

## 6. In-app purchases

Instant Framer Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The app asks Google Play whether you own Pro, on your device; we never receive your payment details, and we don't keep purchase records on servers of our own. Google Play's handling of your purchase is covered by Google's privacy policy.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

We don't keep any of your data on our own servers. Emails you send us stay in our inbox only as long as needed to help you; ask us and we'll delete them. Framed photos stay on your device until you delete them; uninstalling the app removes its private cache but not the photos saved to your gallery. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID at any time as described above.

## 8. Children

Instant Framer is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help address it.

## 9. Security

Photos are processed on your device and never transmitted by the app. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
